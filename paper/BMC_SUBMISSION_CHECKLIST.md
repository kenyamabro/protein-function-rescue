# BMC Bioinformatics submission checklist

Target: **BMC Bioinformatics — Research Article** (computational method and software).

Official guidance checked 14 July 2026:

- Journal scope and submission guidance: https://link.springer.com/journal/12859/submission-guidelines
- Research Article requirements: https://link.springer.com/journal/12859/submission-guidelines/research-article
- Springer Nature generative-AI guidance: https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-our-researchers-and-communities

## Adaptations completed

- Structured abstract with Background, Results and Conclusions; under 350 words.
- Three to ten keywords.
- Research Article sections covering Background, Methods, Results, Discussion and Conclusions.
- Two-sided exact McNemar test for the paired exact-EC benchmark outcomes.
- Generative-AI use documented in Methods, naming Anthropic Claude and OpenAI Codex and describing their roles.
- Complete Declarations heading with ethics, consent, availability, competing interests, funding, authors' contributions and acknowledgements.
- Software availability metadata: project name, repository, operating systems, language and license.
- Submission DOCX generated with double line spacing, continuous line numbering, page numbering and 1-inch margins.

## Required author actions before submission

- Replace the author, affiliation and correspondence placeholders on manuscript lines 3–5.
- Replace `[Student Name]` and the coauthor/supervisor instruction in Authors' contributions.
- Replace the Funding bracket with the actual funder statement or the supplied no-specific-funding statement.
- Replace the Acknowledgements bracket after obtaining permission from anyone named, or write `Not applicable`.
- Replace the placeholder author in `CITATION.cff` and add an ORCID if available.
- Create a versioned GitHub release, archive it in Zenodo, and replace `pending Zenodo DOI` in the Availability statement.
- Ensure every listed author approves the final manuscript and submission.
- Confirm APC funding or request any eligible pricing support at submission. The journal listed an APC of £2,290 / US$3,090 / €2,590 when checked; the amount applicable at acceptance may differ.
